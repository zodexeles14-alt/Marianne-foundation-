Village Savings & Loans (VSLA).

Empowering over 50 women's savings groups and 900+ participants across Ibanda District and beyond through self-managed financial literacy and group funding.

Building financial resilience from the grassroots up.

Village Savings and Loans Associations (VSLAs) are validated micro-finance tools. They are self-managed, community-based savings groups that exist all over the world.

Currently, Marianne Foundation has over 50 groups comprised of over 900 participants. At inception, each group undergoes a rigorous savings, loaning, financial literacy, and budgeting course before receiving seed micro-loans to start sustainable income-generating projects.
